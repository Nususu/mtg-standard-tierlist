Edge = a deck's winner's metagame share ÷ its own total metagame share (share of published decks). It's a point estimate with a 95% confidence interval - only flagged OVER/UNDER when the whole interval clears 1.0, not just the point estimate. MTGDecks columns are an independent cross-check (their meta share is broader than our tournament-only sample, so their edge isn't compressed toward 1 the way ours can be) but carry no confidence interval of their own - the OVER/UNDER flags here are based on our CI-gated edge only.
